# Project Context: Eka Aerospace & Space Research Platform

## Vision
A modern, cinematic, and professional aerospace and space research organization platform. It combines research papers, academic verification, events, projects, mentorship, and e-commerce into a single ecosystem.

**Inspiration:** NASA, ESA, ResearchGate.

## Core Roles
1. **Guest User**: Public content browsing, blurred premium previews.
2. **Regular User**: Profile management, bookmarks, event registration, applications, community participation.
3. **Premium User**: Full access to research papers, PDF downloads, datasets, store discounts.
4. **Academic Premium User**: Institutional verified access (free premium).
5. **Researcher**: Content creation (papers, projects), mentorship. Requires admin approval for publishing.
6. **Admin**: Full platform control, verification, moderation, CMS management.

## Key Features
- **Research System**: Public/Premium/Private papers, PDF/Dataset support, Blur preview for non-premium.
- **Academic Verification**: Manual review for .edu/.ac.in users.
- **Event Management**: Workshops, conferences, seat registration.
- **Gallery**: Space photography, research media.
- **Projects**: Collaborative tracking and showcase.
- **Store**: Merch and educational products.
- **Admin CMS**: Comprehensive management of all entities.
